# DarkCipher MaskedMail 0.1.7 (2026-10-20)

- **New: throwaway addresses.** Masked addresses that end themselves.
  - **Making one:**
    - **⏱ Throwaway ▾** in the header offers 10 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or a custom lifetime (`45m`,
      `6h`, `3d`).
    - **Ctrl+Shift+T**, or the tray menu, makes one with the default lifetime.
    - The address gets its own folder, and is copied to the clipboard.
  - **Live inbox:**
    - checks for mail every 10 s;
    - picks out verification codes (click to copy) and confirmation links (open or copy), skipping footers, social
      links and images;
    - has +1 hour, +1 day, Keep it and Burn now.
  - **In the main window:** the address list shows ⏱ time left on each throwaway, and its card has the same quick
    actions.
  - **On expiry:**
    - by default the address is deleted, so mail to it bounces, and its mail is moved to Trash with its folder removed;
    - in **Settings → Throwaway addresses** you can choose to only disable it, or keep its mail;
    - expiry is checked every 30 s and at every start; one that can't be ended is retried, and the reason is shown.
  - **Services:** works with Fastmail, SimpleLogin, addy.io and Firefox Relay. Fastmail is the default.
  - **Add-ons:** new `throwaway.create` (you approve it) and `throwaway.list` methods.
  - **Logging:** every create, extend, keep and end goes to the encrypted security log.
- **Docs:** the user guide, security model, architecture and add-on docs are updated.
- **Self-test: 49 checks**, with 5 new throwaway checks and 2 new renders.
